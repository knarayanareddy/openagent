# Morning brief

1. Read today's calendar and anything the person flagged yesterday.
2. List active goals that are due or blocked (use the goals block).
3. Reply with at most five bullets: meetings, one decision needed, one risk.
4. If there is nothing worth reading, reply exactly [quiet].

Settings (from the person's adoption): `max_bullets`, `include_goals`.
